## Shared art direction

- Visible pixelated illustrated sprites, soft atmospheric light and dimensional shadows.
- Small faceless cyan-white framed-core player, brighter than environmental accents.
- Two-block-high walls: intact reinforced blue-gray indestructible walls versus pale cracked/chipped breakable walls.
- Sparse, connected organic ground network across the whole map, with quiet fine branches.
- The four biomes differ visually only in the ground network. Walls, plants, crystals, underlying ground palette and atmosphere share the same style.
- Map layout variations are proposals, not new biome-specific materials or props.
